# Customer Churn Analysis & Insights

## Executive Summary (Top 3 Findings)
* **Month-to-Month Risk:** Customers on month-to-month contracts make up 54.8% of the customer base and have a very high churn rate of around 84% to 86%, making this group the main source of customer loss[cite: 7].
* **Early Lifecycle Risk:** Customer churn is most common during the early part of the customer journey. Churned customers have a median tenure of 10 months, compared with 18 months for customers who remain with the service[cite: 7].
* **Impact of Tech Support:** Technical support has a positive effect on retention. For customers on One-Year plans, churn falls from about 68% to 52% when technical support is provided[cite: 7].

---

## Methodology & Visualizations
* **Data Quality:** The dataset is generally clean, with only 1.25% of values missing. These missing values are found only in the `Total_Charges` column[cite: 7].
* **Tenure Distribution:** Customer tenure is right-skewed, indicating that a significant part of the customer base is concentrated within the first 0–15 months[cite: 7].
* **Visualizations:** The EDA uses distribution plots to examine customer tenure, stacked bar charts to compare contract types with churn status, and comparison charts to understand the effect of support services.

---

## Recommended Strategic Interventions
* **Contract Migration:** Provide targeted discounts, incentives, or introductory benefits to encourage high-risk month-to-month customers to move to one-year or two-year contracts[cite: 7].
* **Early-Stage Onboarding:** Introduce automated check-ins and customer success activities during the important 0-to-12-month period to help customers understand the value of the service earlier[cite: 7].
* **Enhanced Support Bundling:** Include premium technical support with selected mid-tier plan renewals to increase the overall value of the subscription and reduce the chances of early churn[cite: 7].
